Each CV is parsed into fields (contact, roles, dates, skills, education), split into chunks that keep their role and company, and embedded. Ask a question or paste a job description and the answer comes back with the exact CV lines that support it.

01 · Parse

Structure the CV

Sections, roles and date ranges are extracted; total experience merges overlapping jobs; 50+ skills are matched and tagged as used in a role or only listed.

02 · Chunk

Context-aware chunks

Every bullet is stored with its role and company, so “Deployed YOLOv8…” still knows it happened at Vantage Retail.

03 · Retrieve

Hybrid search

Questions are embedded with bge-small and compared to every chunk, blended with BM25 keyword scores so exact terms still count.

04 · Ground

Cite the evidence

Answers and job-match scores point to the CV lines they came from, highlighted in the document.
